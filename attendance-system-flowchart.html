Attendance Monitoring System Flowchart

flowchart TD
    A([Start]) --> B[Open login page]
    B --> C[Enter credentials]
    C --> D{Credentials valid?}
    D -->|No| E[Show login error]
    E --> C
    D -->|Yes| F[Java backend validates account against MySQL]
    F --> G{User role?}

    G -->|Admin| H[Open Admin Dashboard]
    G -->|Student| I[Open Student Dashboard]

    subgraph ADMIN[Admin workflow]
        H --> J{Choose admin action}

        J -->|Manage students| K[Add, update, or deactivate student]
        K --> L[Validate and save student data to MySQL]
        L --> J

        J -->|Mark attendance| M[Select date, class, and subject]
        M --> N{Weekend, holiday, or suspension?}
        N -->|Yes| O[Do not create attendance records]
        O --> J
        N -->|No| P[Load subject roster and existing records]
        P --> Q[Mark each student Present, Late, or Absent]
        Q --> R{Entries valid?}
        R -->|No| S[Show validation errors]
        S --> Q
        R -->|Yes| T[Save attendance by student, subject, and date in MySQL]
        T --> U[Show save confirmation]
        U --> J

        J -->|Edit attendance| V[Find attendance record]
        V --> W[Update status and record edit details]
        W --> X[Save changes to MySQL]
        X --> J

        J -->|View or print reports| Y[Select report filters]
        Y --> Z[Retrieve attendance data from MySQL]
        Z --> AA[Display report and statistics]
        AA --> AB{Print report?}
        AB -->|Yes| AC[Print report]
        AB -->|No| J
        AC --> J
    end

    subgraph STUDENT[Student workflow]
        I --> AD[Request personal attendance data]
        AD --> AE[Retrieve student's records by subject and date]
        AE --> AF[Display attendance history and statistics]
        AF --> AG[Calculate absence percentage]
        AG --> AH{Absence percentage?}
        AH -->|20% or more| AI[Show Failure/Drop Zone warning]
        AH -->|15% to less than 20%| AJ[Show Grade Penalty Zone warning]
        AH -->|10% to less than 15%| AK[Show Academic Warning Zone warning]
        AH -->|Less than 10%| AL[Show no threshold warning]
        AI --> AM[Display student dashboard]
        AJ --> AM
        AK --> AM
        AL --> AM
    end

    U --> AN{Logout?}
    X --> AN
    J --> AN
    AC --> AN
    AM --> AN
    AN -->|No| AO{Role?}
    AO -->|Admin| J
    AO -->|Student| AD
    AN -->|Yes| AP[End authenticated session]
    AP --> AQ([Return to login page])
